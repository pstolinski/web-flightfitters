What does imagery include?

Aerial imagery is the foundation of the business. Public site copy says all packages include imagery data collection and that the amount collected is effectively unlimited within the mission.

What does video include?

Video starts with the Advanced package and is positioned as useful for houses, groups, and large buildings from an elevated perspective.

What do these packages cost?

Each package starts at the listed amount and final pricing depends on the mission requirements.

What is post-processing?

Post-processing is included across packages to help deliver the highest quality data possible.

What is same-day dispatch?

Same-day dispatch means requesting drone pilots for data collection within 24 hours, subject to availability and compliance with Part 107.

When will I receive my data?

The public site says clients can expect proofs of imagery 48 hours after capture and invoices are due 30 days from receipt.
